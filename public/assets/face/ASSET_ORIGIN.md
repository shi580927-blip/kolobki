# Face parts v03 alpha

Original image generated for Sharobesy with the built-in image-generation tool, 24 October 2026. No third-party sprite pack or franchise character was copied. Selected for alpha visual review after local 3D checks, not declared finished commercial art.

Prompt: grayscale modular cartoon sprite sheet, four single eye shapes, four eyebrow pairs and four compact mouth shapes, independent transparent parts without skin, sphere or backing plate; front view, soft volume, charcoal outlines and ivory eyes/teeth. Skin-coloured lids and contaminated coloured drafts were excluded.

The runtime reads pixel rectangles from features-v03.json, preserves part proportions and bends each patch over the sphere. Full 12-expression animation production remains in the backlog.
